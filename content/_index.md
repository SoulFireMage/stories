Newly built editor site for my stories, allowing me to live update them and work on them without keeping them in Google Docs and transferring them.

Over the next few days the rest of my unfinished stuff will appear here in all of its rambling glory. 

***None of the stories are written using AI help. This is the one area of creativity that I don't believe, for me, AI is right for.***

I think stories should come from my head and my voice, or phone keypad, or keyboard only. This is not a judgment of other people but I know I prefer books written by humans as I've yet to enjoy an AI-written piece of work.
