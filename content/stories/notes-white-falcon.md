*Under the iceberg, personal notes for the story*

*Gawain, :Wain, white falcon.* 

*Enfolded space: creating worlds by unfolding higher dimensions, engraving reality upon the then repacking them. A fabled technology by a species lost to time.* 

*Enfolded Seed: an ultra rare remnant of the Lost people, the only known example: A golden crystal world that was owned by group of colonists who managed to master it's ability to enfold. It was self supporting, needing only the light of any star to support itself. If attacked it's people Enfolded it from view and carried its anchor, smaller than an atom, in a scattering of ftl ships too well armed and manoeuvrable to be captured.* 

*Acuberrie drive \- standard ftl operated by compressed space such that light speed limits were surpassed in ordinary space.* 

*Fold space: a mythical idea speculated to be available to the species that created Enfolded seeds.* 

*Waystations: humanity spread rapidly in the millenia post ftl travel. Thanks to constant warring, the population is widely spread on many types of space station as well as asteroids and anything possessing sufficient gravity and distance from a star not to be an immediate crushing or furnace hazard.* 

*The MC was born in the depths of space, at our most distant waystation to parents who were ordinary mercenaries who had little time for him. However, unbeknownst to them, the moment of his conception coincided with a never seen before fold event that dumped another Seed directly into his genome. This seed was also the size of an atom and replicated itself through the host dna, replacing all its individual atoms with identical ones that were shells, hiding its immense complexity in myriad inner dimensions.* 

*The result was an embryo that look and even scanned no different from human, yet its entire atomic structure was actually a copy of this seed. Each was entangled to all others such that they were still a single seed. Gawain’s very life was woven of atoms who were all one Seed of a lost race.* 

*The consequences didn't show up in early childhood. For all intents and purposes he was as average and ordinary as any other kid. He took combat lessons from his parents, auto lessons front he station Ai tutors, played with other kids in the station and was an average kid amongst many who inhabited the mercenary ran waystation.* 

*This changed when he got spaced during an attack on his station. It was then he discovered a disturbing ability, a confusing one too. He transformed into a small, alien space craft whilst his mind altered to cope with his new senses and body. This ship accidently drew the attackers but proved to be impossible for them to destroy despite Gawain’s inability to fly or fight competently. Rail gun rounds disappeared into its body and energy weapons were deflected or absorbed.* 

*Fortunately for his sanity, he rapidly learnt how to change back before he was discovered and was able to position himself to be rescued. The hectic nature of the attack and the odd way it concluded left him avoiding the more difficult questions his change would otherwise attract.*

*He was thirteen at the time.* 

*It was a few years before he experienced the change again as he'd been unable to discover just what made it happen the first time. At 13 it had been a shock, but his ship body self didn't experience adrenaline or fear the same way. He never confessed to anyone what he could do at the time.* 

*It was years later when he found out how to shift on purpose.*
